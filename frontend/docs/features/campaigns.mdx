# Proactive Campaigns & Teaser Triggers

Don't wait for visitors to ask a question. **Campaigns** let your bot initiate conversations proactively based on user behavior and page context.

---

## Trigger Types

- **Page URL Matching**: Target visitors on specific pages (e.g. `/pricing`, `/checkout`, `/demo`).
- **Time on Page**: Fire a teaser message after a visitor spends 15 or 30 seconds reading a page.
- **Scroll Depth**: Pop up a teaser after the visitor scrolls past 50% or 75% of a blog post or landing page.
- **Exit Intent**: Detect cursor movement toward the browser's close button and offer a helpful discount or prompt.
- **Device Targeting**: Filter campaigns specifically for Desktop or Mobile visitors.

---

## Example Campaigns

### The Pricing Page Assistant
- **Target URL**: `https://yourdomain.com/pricing`
- **Delay**: 10 seconds
- **Teaser Message**: *"👋 Need help choosing the right plan for your team? I can calculate your ROI in 30 seconds."*

### The Checkout Saver
- **Target URL**: `https://yourdomain.com/checkout*`
- **Trigger**: Exit intent or 60 seconds inactivity
- **Teaser Message**: *"Have questions about our refund guarantee before purchasing? Ask me anything!"*
