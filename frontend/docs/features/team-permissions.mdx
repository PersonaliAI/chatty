# Team Roster & Granular Permissions

Chatty supports multi-operator collaboration with fine-grained access control so agents see only what they need, while owners maintain full administrative authority.

---

## Role Matrix

| Role | Accessible Tabs | Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Owner** | All 21 Tabs | Full control over bot settings, billing, BYOK encryption keys, webhooks, and team invites. |
| **Admin** | Configurable (Default: Inbox, Sources, Design, Settings, Voice, Team, Meetings) | Can manage knowledge base, edit bot persona, oversee inbox queues, and invite agents. Cannot access Billing or BYOK keys. |
| **Agent** | Configurable (Default: Inbox) | Can answer live tickets, send messages, claim sessions, and view internal staff notes. |

---

## Granular Tab Permissions

Owners and Admins can toggle individual tab access for each team member:

- `inbox`: Access live chats, take over conversations, mark tickets resolved.
- `sources`: Upload documents, trigger website crawls.
- `design`: Customize widget branding, colors, launcher styles.
- `settings`: Edit system prompts, change AI model parameters.
- `voice`: Monitor voice call logs and voice configuration.
- `team`: View roster and manage member availability.
- `meetings`: View booked meetings calendar and update schedules.

---

## Bookable Calendar Status

In the Team tab, members can be designated as **Bookable**:
- **Bookable Toggle**: Makes the agent eligible to receive appointments from the round-robin scheduler.
- **Book on Own Calendar**: Syncs bookings to the agent's personal Google/Outlook calendar instead of the organization's central calendar.
